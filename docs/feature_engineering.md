# Feature engineering

Generated by `scripts/build_research_docs.py` from the feature catalogue (`quanara/features.py`) and the feature facts (`quanara/feature_facts.py`). Missing-value shares are measured on the training rows of each scope in What-if mode (Competition mode for hotel-observation features).

## Raw inputs and engineered features

**Raw aviation variables** are columns of `flight_data.xlsx` at route-day grain: Total Seats, Total PAX, Total P2P, Total Transfer, Total Transit, Average Weekly Frequency (decoded to flights), Load Factor, Departure Country/City, Airline Name and cabin-level counts. Only the daily rows (from 2023-01-01) feed features; the 2022 monthly rows are never spread over days.

**Engineered features** are computed from those columns after summing route-day rows per day (or per day × departure country for the market scope) on a complete calendar, where a day without a row counts as zero flights. Ratios are always recomputed from summed numerators and denominators. Trailing windows include the current day: a 7-day window on day t covers t−6 … t.

| Group | Features |
|---|---|
| Aviation | `seats`, `p2p`, `pax`, `transfer`, `flights`, `load_factor`, `seats_7d`, `p2p_7d`, `pax_7d`, `transfer_7d`, `flights_7d`, `load_factor_7d`, `seats_28d`, `p2p_28d` |
| Derived | `p2p_share_7d`, `transfer_share_7d`, `seats_per_flight_7d`, `business_seat_share_7d`, `etihad_seat_share_7d`, `n_routes`, `n_cities`, `n_airlines`, `seats_growth_28d`, `p2p_growth_28d` |
| Historical | `seats_prev7d`, `p2p_prev7d`, `guests_lag364`, `guests_lag364_7d` |
| Time | `dow`, `is_weekend`, `month`, `quarter`, `year`, `doy_sin`, `doy_cos` |
| Market | `market` |
| Hotel (competition) | `new_arrivals`, `new_arrivals_7d`, `same_day_guests` |
